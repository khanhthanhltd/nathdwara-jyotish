Data Deletion

Nathdwara Jyotish · Effective date: October 9, 2026

Nathdwara Jyotish does not use an email/password sign-up system — there is no username or password tied to your identity. Your birth details and settings are stored only on your device. Your AI chat history is stored on our AI service provider's server in association with an anonymous, randomly generated device identifier. This page explains how to delete both.

1. Request deletion of your server-side chat history

  1. Email khanhthanhltd@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request — Nathdwara Jyotish".
  2. If you know your device identifier, include it so we can locate your data quickly. If you don't, tell us the approximate dates you used the AI chat feature and the name of the character you chatted with — this helps us locate the right record.
  3. We will delete the chat history associated with your device identifier from our AI service provider's server, and confirm by email, within 30 days.

2. Remove your data from this device right now

You can also remove the copy of your data stored locally on your device immediately, in either of two ways:

Option A — Clear the App's data (keeps the App installed)
  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  3. Select Nathdwara Jyotish.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This immediately erases your locally stored birth details, settings, and the local copy of your chat history from this device. This data cannot be restored afterwards.

Option B — Uninstall the App
  1. Press and hold the Nathdwara Jyotish app icon.
  2. Tap Uninstall (or drag to the Uninstall option).
  3. Confirm.

Uninstalling removes the App and its locally stored data (birth details, settings, and local chat history) from your device immediately. As with Option A, this data cannot be restored afterwards.

Clearing local data or uninstalling the App removes it from your device right away, but does not by itself delete the chat history associated with your device identifier on our AI service provider's server — use the email request in Section 1 to have that copy deleted as well.
