FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no email, password or sign-in.

Does the AI chat need internet?

Yes. Chat messages are sent to our AI service to generate a reply, so an internet connection is required.

Are the readings real astrological advice?

No. Nathdwara Jyotish is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

Is the app connected to a temple in Nathdwara?

No. It is an independent app. Nathdwara and Pichwai refer to the place and the art tradition that inspired its design.

What languages does it support?

English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.

Is Nathdwara Jyotish free?

Yes, it is free to download and use, supported by ads.
